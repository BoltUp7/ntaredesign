Our Mission

Theatre Changes Lives

We believe every child deserves the chance to stand in the spotlight — not just on stage, but in life.

Nitsch Theatre Arts (NTA) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit arts education organization based in Central Illinois. Since our founding, we've been creating professional-quality theatrical experiences where young performers learn to express themselves, collaborate with others, and discover the courage that lives within them.

Our programs reach students from kindergarten through high school, providing affordable access to classes, workshops, summer camps, and full-scale stage productions — regardless of a family's financial situation.

What We Offer

Programs for Every Stage

Whether your child is a first-time performer or a seasoned stage veteran, we have a program that's right for them.

🌱

Little Stars

Ages 5 – 8

An introduction to the magic of theatre through storytelling, movement, and imaginative play. Perfect for our youngest dreamers.

  • Basic acting fundamentals
  • Storytelling & imagination
  • End-of-session showcase
⭐

Rising Stars

Ages 9 – 14

Dive deeper into the craft. Students explore character development, scene study, vocal projection, and collaborative performance.

  • Scene study & character work
  • Voice & movement training
  • Musical theatre elements
  • Full production participation
Most Popular
🌟

Spotlight Ensemble

Ages 14 – 18

Our advanced program for dedicated young artists ready to take on leading roles and develop professional-level performance skills.

  • Advanced scene study
  • Directing & leadership roles
  • Audition preparation
  • Community outreach shows

Not sure which program is right? We'll help you find the perfect fit.
